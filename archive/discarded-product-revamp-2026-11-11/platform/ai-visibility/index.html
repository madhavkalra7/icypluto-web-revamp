Your brand

What it helps you understand.

AI visibility is about understanding how your brand is represented in generated answers. IcyPluto gives marketing teams a place to investigate those answers alongside the questions, competitors, and report settings behind them.

What you can investigate.

Brand mentions

Inspect whether the brand appears in an answer and read how it is described.

Competitor context

Explore which competing brands appear in the same analysis.

Question and market context

Interpret results alongside the prompts, region, and available model settings used for the report.

From question to follow-up.

  1. Start with a useful question.

    Choose a question that reflects a real customer need. Add the brand and competitors you want to investigate.

  2. Read the answer.

    Review individual responses. A mention count becomes more useful when you understand the surrounding context.

  3. Choose a follow-up.

    Investigate cited sources, research another question, or bring a finding into content and website work.

Read the context.
Know the limits.

An answer is a response to a particular question under particular settings. It is not a fixed ranking for your brand across every AI platform. Model availability and report settings depend on the analysis you run. IcyPluto does not guarantee inclusion or recommendations.
