FAQ

A few answers.

What does IcyPluto actually do?

IcyPluto brings AI visibility measurement and marketing tools into one workspace. You can monitor brand mentions, compare competitors, investigate cited sources, and use content and optimization tools to work on what you find.

Is this a replacement for SEO?

It expands the picture beyond traditional search rankings. SEO still matters. AIO and GEO also consider how AI systems understand your brand and include it in generated answers. The CST framework connects content, structure, and technical foundations.

Which AI platforms can I explore?

The product includes model-level views for platforms such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Available models and report settings depend on the analysis you run.

Who is IcyPluto built for?

Brand, content, SEO, and growth teams—and agencies managing client brands—who need to understand how they appear in AI search and turn that information into a plan.

Can a tool guarantee that AI will recommend us?

No. AI responses vary by model, question, region, and time. IcyPluto helps you measure that presence, investigate gaps, and improve the inputs you control. It does not control what a model says.
